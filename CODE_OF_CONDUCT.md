# Community Code of Conduct · 社区行为准则

## Our commitment

ClaudeU Labs 致力于建设一个专业、友善、可验证、尊重隐私的技术社区。任何参与者都不应因经验水平、语言、国籍、地区、年龄、身体状况、性别认同与表达、性取向、种族、宗教或其他个人特征受到骚扰或排斥。

ClaudeU Labs is committed to a professional, welcoming, evidence-based, and privacy-respecting technical community. Participation should be free from harassment or exclusion based on experience level, language, nationality, region, age, disability, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, race, religion, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Distinguish confirmed facts, reproduction evidence, and hypotheses.
- Protect credentials, personal data, customer data, and security-sensitive details.
- Respect repository scope, licenses, trademarks, and third-party ownership.
- Accept maintainer decisions about safety, relevance, duplication, and release boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or personal attacks;
- publishing private information, credentials, logs, profiles, or customer data without authorization;
- impersonation or false claims of affiliation, endorsement, certification, or partnership;
- spam, coordinated inauthentic engagement, paid or automated stars, or unrelated promotion;
- attempts to use the community to bypass account, payment, subscription, access-control, integrity, update, or service-policy requirements;
- knowingly submitting copyrighted, proprietary, or official assets without permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and official community interactions managed by ClaudeU Labs.

Report conduct concerns privately through [ClaudeU Support](https://claudeu.com/zh-CN/support). Do not use a public issue when the report contains personal, security, or abuse-related information. Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users.

Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is not.
